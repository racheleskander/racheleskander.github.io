About

I'm a product designer and creative director based in San Francisco. I believe in design as a tool for clarity — simplifying complexity, building trust, and making technology feel human.

Over the past decade, I've worked with startups and scale-ups to define product vision, ship design systems, and lead teams through ambiguity. My work sits at the intersection of systems thinking and craft.
