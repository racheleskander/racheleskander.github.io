About Me

I am an Associate Regulatory Affairs Specialist at Medtronic Cardiac Surgery. I graduated from the University of Pittsburgh with a B.S. in Bioengineering with a concentration in Medical Product Design and a Certificate in Innovation, Product Design, and Entrepreneurship.

I like challenges and engineering is my outlet to find creative solutions to everyday problems. Whether I’m iterating in CAD, troubleshooting a 3D print, or getting a little dusty with CNC machining, I thrive in the messy, creative gap between a napkin sketch and a physical prototype. I don't just like trying new things, I like mastering the tools that build them. Call me a jack of all masters!
