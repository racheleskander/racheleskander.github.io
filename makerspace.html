Makerspace

Role Program Manager/Design Mentor
Timeline 4 years
Context University of Pittsburgh

As a mentor, I was able to teach/guide students for academic and personal projects using various fabrication techniques and methods. I was especially fulfilled when I began my role as Program Manager, planning and executing workshops, where students can apply their understanding of prototyping to learn advanced techniques and participate in fun projects! Throughout my time as Program Manager, I developed over 40 workshops and trainings to a full class of students and faculty interested in learning more about fabrication! The images on this page are from various workshops and mentoring moments I had during my time.
