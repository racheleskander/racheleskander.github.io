Makerspace

Role Fabrication Liason
Timeline 4 months (1 semester)
Context University of Pittsburgh

In Spring 2023, I participated and assisted in a medical product design class. Although enrolled as a student, my role as Makerspace's Program Manager allowed me to act as a bridge between the teacher and students. I taught and trained students on how to use all prototyping tools and machines needed for the class. We were guided to recreate a blood pressure monitor for the final project. I used machining mills, 3D ender printers, vacuum form molding, silicon molding, 3d finishing techniques, sewing machines, sketching techniques, and AutoDesk Fusion.
