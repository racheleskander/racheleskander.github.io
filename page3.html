Moving the Cities

Role Ideation and Implementation Lead
Timeline Hackathon (2 weeks)
Context University of Pittsburgh/Moving the Cities

I was very lucky to participate in the Motiving the Cities 2023 program in Münster, Germany. Teams were assigned to pitch an idea to address United Nations Goal 17, increasing global partnerships. My team and I proposed a mobile technology hub equipped with Wi-Fi and computers that will drive to rural villages of Pando, Bolivia to teach children digital literacy skills. It was such a valuable and unique experience because not only did we have a short timeline to ideate and pitch, but also, I was working with the most diverse group I have ever encountered
